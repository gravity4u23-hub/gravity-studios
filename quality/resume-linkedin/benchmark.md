## Our offer against this (honest)

**Where we win**
- **Price:** $25 / $59 / $89 vs $40–$150 for the four comparable resume + LinkedIn Fiverr sellers above (not the market floor: resume-only gigs exist at $5–$20, per Atlas Nov 23) and $149 entry at TopResume (before LinkedIn add-ons). Claim the price beat only against comparable resume + LinkedIn sellers.
- **Speed:** 2–3 days vs 3–7 days for three of the four Fiverr sellers and about a week at TopResume. **Maya S delivers in 1 day [S], so we do not beat the fastest.**
- **LinkedIn included at $59;** TopResume sells it as an add-on.

**Where we lose, said straight**
- **No track record:** 0 reviews vs 35–523 for the Fiverr sellers. On Fiverr, that is the main reason buyers pick someone else.
- **No certified writer.** Never imply one.
- **The complaint hits us:** our drafting is AI-assisted. The gig does not say so yet. Buyers' #1 complaint is AI-sounding, template output — so the fix has to be visible, not a promise.
- **No interview promise, ever** (complaint 6). Nobody controls hiring.

**The complaint fix we must prove in the gold sample (step 2)**
1. **Targeted, not template:** every bullet tied to the buyer's own 1–2 job posts (keywords from the post, not a stock list).
2. **Nothing invented, checked by a script:** every number, title, employer and date in the rewrite must appear in the buyer's original resume or answers — the same invented-number check the Listing Makeover eval uses (Gravity-Studio PR #52). Prototyped this run: `resume_lint.py` (self-tests 9/9) fails any number, year, % or $ not in the original, and any stock phrase the buyer didn't use; warns when fewer than 3 job-post keywords appear. Limit: it can't catch invented words-only facts ("led a team"), so a person still reads every line.
3. **Say AI up front:** add "AI-assisted drafting, every line edited and checked by a person" to the gig, same as the Wellness Blog fix.
4. **Plain words test:** no stock phrases ("results-driven", "dynamic", "proven track record") — a lint list, like `review_lint`'s canned-reply check.
