BUYER ANSWERS (SYNTHETIC) — the 6 questions we ask before writing. Anything the rewrite says must come from the resume above or from these answers.

Q1. What did you train the new cashiers on?
A: The register, returns and exchanges, and the closing checklist. I check on each of them during their first week.

Q2. When the manager is off, what are you in charge of?
A: The whole store for the shift. Breaks, call-outs, and moving people to the registers where the lines are longest.

Q3. How do your cash counts come out?
A: My counts have never come up short when the manager re-checked them.

Q4. Do you have numbers for the credit card sign-ups?
A: No, I don't remember the numbers. I was top of the store some months.

Q5. Do the endcaps follow a plan?
A: Yes, they follow the weekly ad plan from corporate.

Q6. Anything with payroll or hours?
A: When I make the schedule I have to keep it under the labor hours the manager gives me.
