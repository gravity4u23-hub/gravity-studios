## Our offer against this (honest)

**Where we win**
- **Price against agencies:** $20 for 500 words vs ~$25–$60 at Gengo.
- **Scope no one above sells:** a menu + service pages + 5 Google Business posts kit ($79), formatted like the original, with a "terms to confirm" list (dish names, local words). None of the 5 Fiverr gigs read mentions menus or Google posts.
- **Speed:** 1 day on Basic matches the fastest (Lubin, Leo).

**Where we lose, said straight**
- **Price on Fiverr:** we cost **2–4× more** than four sellers with 41–1,627 five-star reviews ($5–$10 vs $20 for ~500 words). We have 0 reviews.
- **The #1 complaint hits us hardest of any service:** our drafting is AI-assisted, and **no one on the team is a native Spanish speaker**. Competitors sell "native speaker, no Google Translate, no ChatGPT" as their main promise. If a buyer finds our text reads like machine output, that is the review that sinks the gig.
- **No one can check the Spanish for us today.** Claude writes fluent Spanish, but the gate rule is a fluent person reads it. Atlas and the owner don't. That is a gap, not a detail.

**The complaint fix (what must be true before this gig lists)**
1. **Say AI up front**, in the gig and in every delivery: "AI-assisted drafting, every line reviewed by [name]". Never "manual" or "human translation".
2. **A fluent Spanish reader reviews every delivery** and is named in the note. **This is the open question for the owner** (see below): who is that person.
3. **Checked by a script** (prototyped this run): `spanish_check.py` fails a Spanish version whose prices, times or phone number differ from the original, that drops a line, that leaves a line in English, that drops an allergen (peanut, shellfish, milk…), or whose delivery note lacks the AI line, the reviewer's name or the "terms to confirm" list. Self-tests 12/12. Limit: it cannot tell natural Spanish from stiff Spanish — that is item 2.
4. **Sell the kit, not the per-word job.** We can't win a $5-per-400-words race. The Premium kit (menu + pages + Google posts) for a local business is the only package with no direct competitor here.
