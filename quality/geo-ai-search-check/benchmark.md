## What buyers complain about (evidence)
**Honest gap: no verbatim buyer review of a GEO audit was found this run.** The two Fiverr gigs show no low-star text in what loaded. The one Trustpilot page tried (myaicampaigns.com) has a single 5★ review. Grok or Atlas should pull 3 real low-star reviews from Fiverr GEO gigs in a browser before Atlas scores row 6.

What exists is the industry's own warning list, written by sellers [V]. Treat it as "what a careful buyer is told to watch for", not as buyer complaints:
1. **Guaranteed placement.** "They guarantee a specific position in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews. Nobody controls those answers." (edthi.com, Nov 6, 2026 [V]). eseospace.com (Oct 13, 2026 [V]) quotes OpenAI: "advertisers have no ability to shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT's responses."
2. **A score with nothing behind it.** "Their report is one visibility score with no prompts, answers or sources behind it." (edthi #2)
3. **No sources named.** "They can't tell you which sources ChatGPT cites for your category." (edthi #3)
4. **No crawler check.** "They don't check whether AI crawlers can reach your site." (edthi #7)
5. **Long contracts before a baseline.** "They insist on a 12-month contract before showing any baseline." (edthi #8)
6. **Software scoring you can't check.** The SE Ranking review [V] says G2 reviewers call Otterly's sentiment classifier oversensitive, with "a plainly neutral mention as negative", and that Otterly "hasn't published an accuracy figure for its readiness scoring." (Second-hand: we did not open the G2 reviews.)

## Our offer against this (honest)
**Where we win**
- **Evidence (complaint 2):** every check is a screenshot of the actual answer, for questions written before the first check. The free tools give a score in 60 seconds. They do not show the buyer the exact answer for the buyer's own customer question. Whether Insites shows the answers behind its score is *not shown* on its page.
- **No promises (complaint 1):** the offer already bans "will rank higher" and similar lines.
- **No contract (complaint 5):** one-time, no subscription.
- **Speed vs human audits:** 2 days vs 3 days for both Fiverr audits above.

**Where we lose, said straight**
- **Price.** $149 is about 3× the best Fiverr audits ($40–$50), and the free tools cost $0. Only our Fiverr Basic ($25) is cheaper than a human audit, and it is 3 checks, not 9.
- **Speed vs tools.** The free tools answer in under 60 seconds.
- **Gaps the warning list names that our runbook skips:**
  - **No crawler check (complaint 4).** Fixed in this PR as a prototype: `robots_ai_check.py` reads the buyer's public robots.txt and reports whether ChatGPT search, Perplexity and Google's crawlers are blocked. It never calls Google-Extended an AI Overview blocker and never says "allowed" means "will be named". `--test` 12/12.
  - **Sources not recorded (complaint 3).** The screenshots often show the cited sources, but the runbook doesn't ask us to write them down. Proposed: one line per check, "sources the engine cited", added in step 2.
  - **ChatGPT business profile not checked.** It is new (Nov 10, 2026) and free. Proposed fix line: "Claim your free ChatGPT business profile so your details are correct". **Wording must say this fixes accuracy, not placement,** per OpenAI's own line.

**What this means for the gate (step 3 asks: beat or match the best on quality AND beat on price or speed)**
- vs **human Fiverr audits**: we can beat them on quality (screenshot evidence + the 3 additions above) and we beat them on speed (2 days vs 3). Price loses at $149; the $25 Fiverr Basic wins on price.
- vs **free tools**: we lose on price and speed. The only case for paying is the evidence, the buyer's own questions, and a person turning it into 3 fixes. That case must be shown in the gold sample, not claimed.
- **Pitch rule (same as the Review Desk):** never claim we are cheaper or faster than the free tools, and never sell the audit as if no free check exists.
