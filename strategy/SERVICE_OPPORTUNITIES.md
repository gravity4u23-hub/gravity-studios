# Service Opportunities — provide value, get paid

Owner direction, Nov 21, 2026: *find services people need — a system that doesn't exist yet, or an existing one made far better. No shoveling, nothing to buy or stock. We provide and get paid. Think of many different services.*

## The filter every idea must pass
1. **$0 to start.** No inventory, no ads, no paid tools before the first sale.
2. **AI does the labor.** Claude/Atlas/Grok produce the deliverable; the owner only approves and sends.
3. **Remote.** Delivered by email/Docs/links from Flagstaff to anywhere.
4. **A pain people already pay to fix** (or lose money from), so the pitch is "we do it better/faster/cheaper", not "you should want this".
5. **Recurring or ladders into recurring.** One-off is fine as a door opener.
6. **Honest.** No guarantees we can't control (rankings, sales), no medical/legal/financial advice.

## Recommendation: build #1 and #2 next
- **Review Reply Desk (20/20).** Every local business gets reviews; most answer few or none, and unanswered bad reviews cost customers. It's the same buyer list as the GEO/GBP audits, so one prospecting pass sells three things. Delivery: the owner of the business adds us as a manager on their Google profile (their tap, their account — we never ask for passwords), or we send replies in a doc for them to paste. Recurring from month one.
- **Listing Makeover (19/20).** Sellers know their listings underperform but hate writing. We already know crystal/wellness products cold (Davias, Blog Engine). Fast proof: we makeover Davias's own listings first as the demo.

## How each one gets validated before we build much
1. Write the one-page offer + one real sample (for our own businesses where possible — Davias listings, the Gravity site).
2. Find 10 named public prospects with a visible version of the pain (e.g. 15 unanswered reviews; listings with 5-word descriptions).
3. Owner taps approve → 10 pitches.
4. **Kill rule:** 0 paid after 10 pitches in 14 days → pause and rethink price or buyer, keep the template.

## Rules that carry over
No guaranteed results, no fake reviews ever (we reply to real ones; we never write reviews), no impersonation of the business without its written OK, no logins or passwords — the client grants access through the platform's own manager/permission feature.
