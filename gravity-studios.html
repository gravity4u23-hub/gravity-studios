Payments & policies

How paying works — in plain English

Paying

One-time services are paid up front through a secure Stripe payment link or a Stripe invoice. Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. PayPal is accepted on request. We never take card details by email or phone.

Subscriptions

Blog Engine and Missed-Call Rescue subscriptions are managed by you in the Stripe customer portal — update your payment method or cancel any time, no phone call required. If a payment fails, Stripe retries it automatically and lets you know.

Questions & refunds

Billing question, or something not right with a delivery? Email gravity4u23@gmail.com and we'll sort it out with you directly. Every delivery includes a video walkthrough so you can see exactly what you paid for.
